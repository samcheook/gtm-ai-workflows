# End-of-Day Update

Generate a short summary of what was accomplished today. Designed for async team updates or manager check-ins.

## When to use

- End of each working day
- Before logging off
- When a manager asks "what did you get done today?"

## Inputs

- Today's session activity (calls made, emails sent, research completed)
- Calendar events attended
- Completed to-do items
- Any notable wins or blockers encountered

## Execution

### Step 1: Review Today's Activity

Scan for:
- Meetings attended and outcomes
- Emails sent (especially prospect outreach or AE handoffs)
- Research briefs completed
- Calls made and results
- Pipeline movement (new opportunities, meetings booked)
- Blockers hit or resolved

### Step 2: Identify Top 2-3 Accomplishments

Pick the 2-3 things that moved the needle most. Prioritise:
1. Revenue-generating activity (meetings booked, pipeline created)
2. Relationship-building (AE handoffs, prospect replies)
3. Operational progress (research completed, process improvements)

### Step 3: Note Blockers or Carry-Forwards

Anything that didn't get done and why. Anything that needs attention tomorrow.

### Step 4: Generate Update

Format as 2-3 plain-English dot points. Written for a manager or team channel.

```
## EOD — [Date]

* [What you accomplished — specific outcome, not activity]
* [Second accomplishment]
* [Blocker or carry-forward if any]
```

## Output Format

2-3 bullet points. Plain language. Specific outcomes, not vague activity descriptions.

**Good:** "Booked discovery call with [Company] CFO for Thursday. Sent research brief to AE."
**Bad:** "Worked on outreach and research tasks."

## Constraints

- Max 3 bullet points. If nothing significant happened, say "admin/prep day, no major outputs."
- Focus on outcomes, not effort.
- Don't inflate small tasks into accomplishments.
- Write for someone scanning quickly, not reading deeply.
